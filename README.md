## Revision 2 — force-organisation cleanup

Native selectable units are grouped into the standard HH1 force slots:

- HQ: Slaugth Intendent
- Elites: Psyhunter Slaugth, Herdtalon Slaugth, Relic Hunter Slaugth, Voidling Jumpers
- Troops: Indoctrinated Armsman, Voidling Darklights
- Fast Attack: Corkirya Irad Drone
- Heavy Support: Necro Harvester Drone
- Lords of War: The Worm that Walks

Barasonilash, Baalite / Fire Scorpion and Cythor Fiend are no longer exposed as loose No Force Org root entries; they remain available as Herdtalon companion upgrades. Borrowed faction subsets remain available under their force-slot categories.
